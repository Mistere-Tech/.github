### :wave: Who we are

Mistere Tech is an independent software studio from Ascoli Piceno, Italy.
We build desktop apps that solve one everyday problem well: they work offline, keep your files on your computer,
and never do anything you did not ask for.

- :camera: **Photography** and :racing_car: **motors** are where our ideas come from
- :lock: **Your data stays yours**: no accounts, no cloud, AI that runs on your machine
- :shield: **Safe by design**: every copy verified, nothing deleted without your confirmation
- :it: :gb: **Italian and English** interfaces from day one
